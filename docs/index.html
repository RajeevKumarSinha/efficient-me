⚡ Efficient Me

100% Local-First & Zero-Telemetry
Last Updated: August 27, 2026
Core Privacy Commitment: Efficient Me ("we", "our", or "the app") is built from the ground up with a Zero-Cloud, 100% Local-First Data Sovereignty architecture. Your productivity, personal habits, energy scores, and daily tasks are private to you and never leave your physical device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Efficient Me does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, behavioral analytics, location data, or device identifiers to external servers or third parties.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All data you input—including:

  • Tasks, periodic chores, and priority deadlines
  • Daily energy and mood ratings
  • Elastic habit streak logs
  • Long-term OKR milestone goals
  • Circadian chronotype preferences

is stored strictly in an encrypted local SQLite database housed inside your device's isolated application sandbox.

3. Backups & Portability

You can create AES-256 encrypted backups protected by your personal passphrase. Exported JSON and CSV files remain completely under your control and are saved directly to your device storage or shared via your system share sheet.

4. Device Permissions Used

Efficient Me requests only the minimal device permissions required for core functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To deliver local task reminders, circadian peak check-in notifications, and habit reminders on your device.
  • VIBRATE: To provide haptic feedback during focus sprints, somatic breathwork pacing, and button interactions.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore your local reminder schedules if your device restarts.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Efficient Me contains:

  • 0 Tracking SDKs (No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook Pixel)
  • 0 Advertising SDKs (No AdMob, Unity Ads, or programmatic ad networks)
  • 0 Cloud Database Connections (No user accounts or cloud syncing)

6. Children's Privacy

Because Efficient Me collects zero personal data, it does not collect information from children or any other user demographic.

7. Data Erasure & Factory Reset

You maintain 100% control over your data at all times. You can permanently erase all tasks, habits, and history at any moment by navigating to: ⚙️ Settings → Erase All Local Data.

8. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via GitHub repository issues at github.com/RajeevKumarSinha/efficient-me.
